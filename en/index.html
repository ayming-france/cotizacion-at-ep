Context

The situation of companies in Spain

You overpay for a classification nobody has reviewed since

Spain's Supreme Court has been clear since 2018: each employee contributes for what they actually do, not for the company's declared activity, under Supreme Court rulings (STS, Sentencias del Tribunal Supremo) 1550/2018 and 762/2019. With the new premium schedule and the move to the National Classification of Economic Activities (CNAE-2025), the whole reviewable period now falls under these rules.

The gap between the rate the company applies and the one the Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social (TGSS, the Social Security treasury) considers correct ends in inspection reports, penalties or litigation, and a wrong classification means overpaying every month.

At Ayming we believe a company should neither fear an inspection report nor pay more than it owes. The same doctrine that raises the driver's rate to 6.70% lowers the office engineer's to 1.50%, and we apply it both ways.

Where the CNAE classification and the right AT/EP contribution diverge

Acquisitions and mergers

Same job, two rates. Each deal brings in contribution accounts with their original codes and classifications, and employees move between sites and companies without their account following them.

Monthly settlement

Inherited criteria, never checked. The direct settlement system proposes each month and the company confirms. The occupation codes set on the hiring date pass from one company to another, and from one payroll team to the next, without anyone reviewing them.

2026 regulatory changes

Rates that rise unnoticed. Cuadro II, the occupation table, has been shortened and the removed occupations revert to the activity rate. And a company that did not report its CNAE-2025 code is automatically charged the highest rate in its family.

For industrial groups that want to comply with the rules and pay the right amount of social contributions, Ayming International HR reviews the AT/EP classification, recovers the amounts overpaid and corrects the system settings, on a success fee basis.

AT/EP contribution rates

  • Activity mapping

    Analyse your practices and identify the activities actually carried out at each site.

  • Occupation mapping

    Identify the Cuadro II occupations that can give rise to a specific AT/EP rate, different from the one derived from the CNAE.

  • Impact quantification

    Compare the rate actually applied with the one that should have applied, and quantify historical overpayments and future savings.

  • Claim and correction

    Prepare the refund and correction claims before the Seguridad Social, with supporting documentation.

  • Follow-up of claims and litigation

    Follow administrative or court decisions through to their actual enforcement, coordinating with the competent bodies, with legal support in case of disputes or litigation.

Documents required

What we need to start the study.

General

  • List of entities, work sites and contribution account codes (CCC, códigos cuenta de cotización), with current and historical CNAE, and of the mergers, acquisitions and reorganisations that affected the scope analysed
  • Historical employee master file: ID, entity/CCC, site, job, dates of hire/exit/job change and occupation code applied
  • Payroll extract for the last 4 years (Excel/CSV format)
  • Contribution settlement receipt (RLC, Recibo de Liquidación de Cotizaciones) for each settlement period
  • Calculation and contribution base files from the direct settlement system (SLD, Sistema de Liquidación Directa), if available

AT/EP rates

  • Job descriptions, with their occupational risk assessments
  • Documentation of the activity carried out at each site: activity licences, activity descriptions, occupational risk prevention (PRL) documentation, any document that shows the functional and operational separation of the activities
